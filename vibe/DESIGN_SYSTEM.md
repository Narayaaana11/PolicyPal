# PolicyPal Luxury Design System & Aesthetic Direction

## Color Palette Tokens (User Enforced)
- **Ink Black** (`--ink-black`: `#001219`): Deep background canvas and dark surfaces.
- **Dark Teal** (`--dark-teal`: `#005F73`): Container fills and ambient glow gradient stops.
- **Dark Cyan** (`--dark-cyan`: `#0A9396`): Glassmorphism borders and interactive glow rings.
- **Pearl Aqua** (`--pearl-aqua`: `#94D2BD`): Secondary text, metadata badges, and high-contrast links.
- **Vanilla Custard** (`--vanilla-custard`: `#E9D8A6`): Warm luxury accents and highlight indicators.
- **Golden Orange** (`--golden-orange`: `#EE9B00`): Primary CTA button gradients and high-value metric highlights.
- **Burnt Caramel** (`--burnt-caramel`: `#CA6702`): Secondary button gradients and hover glows.
- **Rusty Spice** (`--rusty-spice`: `#BB3E03`): Secondary warning fills.
- **Oxidized Iron** (`--oxidized-iron`: `#AE2012`): Alert container borders and disclaimer highlights.
- **Brown Red** (`--brown-red`: `#9B2226`): Deep alert container fills.

## Typography
- **Display Font**: Playfair Display (Serif, 800 weight) for luxury high-engineering headlines.
- **Body Font**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Clean geometric sans, 400/500 weight) for readability.
- **Mono Font**: JetBrains Mono (Monospace) for policy numbers, dates, and value scores.

## Applied Stacks
- **Next.js Web App (`/web`)**: Next.js 14 App Router, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Framer Motion animations.
- **Flutter Mobile App (`/app`)**: Material 3 Dark Theme with bespoke Ink Black & Golden Orange luxury palette.
